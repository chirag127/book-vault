# 📖 Make It Stick — Reading Edition

> [!QUOTE]
> "Learning is deeper and more durable when it's effortful. Learning that's easy is like writing in sand, here today and gone tomorrow."

## Executive Overview

Written by two of the world's leading memory researchers ([[Henry L. Roediger III]] and [[Mark A. McDaniel]], Washington University in St. Louis) and storyteller [[Peter C. Brown]], *Make It Stick* distills a decade-long collaboration among eleven cognitive scientists into a single governing claim: **the study methods that feel most effective—rereading, highlighting, cramming, massed repetition—are largely ineffective, while the methods that feel hardest—self-testing, spaced practice, interleaving—produce durable, flexible mastery.**

The book dismantles the **fluency illusion** (familiarity mistaken for knowledge), then rebuilds learning practice around seven evidence-backed techniques: retrieval practice, spacing, interleaving, varied practice, elaboration, generation, and reflection—bound together by metacognitive calibration and a growth-oriented view of ability.

## Core Takeaway

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Get it out to get it in.** Memory is strengthened by *retrieving* knowledge under conditions of mild struggle—not by re-exposing yourself to it. Design your practice so that success is never guaranteed, then let feedback correct the errors.
