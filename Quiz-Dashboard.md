## How to Take a Quiz

1. Open any book directory (e.g. `Make-It-Stick/`)
2. Open the `Quiz.md` file inside it
3. Switch to **Reading View** (Ctrl+E)
4. Click options to answer, then hit **Submit Quiz**
5. Your score is saved automatically here

## Tips

- Aim for **70%+** to consider a book's concepts well understood
- Re-take quizzes after 1 week using spaced repetition principles
- Use the [[Make-It-Stick/Quiz|Make It Stick Quiz]] to start
