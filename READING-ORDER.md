> Within each pillar, books are ordered by curriculum number inside their level-2 category; see [[SUBCATEGORY-MAP]], [[ALL-BOOKS]], and the dashboards in [[MOCs/00-MASTER-INDEX|MOCs]].

## Important design rule

A book may link to books in later pillars when the connection is meaningful, but its primary pillar remains the pillar where it is most useful in this curriculum. Wikilinks create a graph; the numbered pillars create a reliable path through that graph. Every book note carries a `## Related Books` section with at least three computed wikilinks and a `next_reads` front-matter list, so the graph stays connected even before the vault is fully generated.
