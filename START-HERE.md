# Start Here

If you are new to the vault, do not begin with a random popular book. Begin by learning how to learn — then follow a goal-based path.

## First sequence

1. [[Make-It-Stick|Make It Stick]] — why retrieval and spaced practice matter.
2. [[A-Mind-for-Numbers|A Mind for Numbers]] — how focused and diffuse modes support difficult learning.
3. [[How-to-Read-a-Book|How to Read a Book]] — inspectional, analytical, and comparative reading.
4. [[How-to-Take-Smart-Notes|How to Take Smart Notes]] — the note-taking method this vault is built on.
5. [[Ultralearning|Ultralearning]] — design an intensive self-directed learning project.
6. [[Peak|Peak]] — deliberate practice and the role of feedback.
7. [[Range|Range]] — why broad exploration supports later specialization.
8. [[Deep-Work|Deep Work]] — deep concentration as the foundation of valuable work.

After that sequence, continue through [[READING-ORDER]], use [[SUBCATEGORY-MAP]] to pick categories, and browse the live dashboards in [[MOCs/00-MASTER-INDEX|MOCs]].
